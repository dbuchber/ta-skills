# Austria: regulations and standards checklist

**Verified:** 2 November 2026, against primary sources (Austrian Standards shop, OVE, OIB, RIS,
Land Oberösterreich, sanierungsoffensive.gv.at). Example state: **Upper Austria (OÖ)** – other
states have their own building and air-quality law; check their RIS equivalents.

**How to use this file.** It maps documents to planning steps. It does **not** summarise their
content (standards are copyrighted, and a summary would be wrong in detail). Before relying on an
item, check the current edition – standards and funding change several times a year. This is not
legal advice; responsibility lies with the licensed installer (and the electrician for the
mains connection).

Sources for checking: [Austrian Standards shop](https://www.austrian-standards.at/de/shop),
[OVE shop](https://shop.ove.at), [OIB guidelines](https://www.oib.or.at/de/oib-richtlinien),
[RIS state law](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at).

## Which step needs which document

| Planning step | Documents |
|---|---|
| Heat load, generator size | ÖNORM EN 12831-1 + **ÖNORM H 7500-1:2015** |
| Hydraulics, safety equipment, buffer sizing | ÖNORM EN 12828 + **ÖNORM H 12828:2023** (replaces H 5151-1), **ÖNORM H 5142:2020** (hydraulic circuits), boiler manual per ÖNORM EN 303-5 |
| Boiler room, fuel store | OIB-RL 2, **ÖNORM H 5170:2016**, ÖNORM EN ISO 20023:2019 (pellet storage) |
| Flue, combustion air | OIB-RL 3, Oö. Fangverordnung, chimney sweep |
| Filling water, antifreeze | **ÖNORM H 5195-1:2024**, **H 5195-2:2025** |
| Pipe insulation | **ÖNORM H 5155:2025** |
| Domestic hot water, legionella | **ÖNORM B 1921:2023** (replaces B 5019), ÖNORM B 2531:2025 + EN 806 |
| Solar thermal | **ÖNORM H 7777:2023**, EN 12975:2022, EN 12977-1:2018 |
| Electrical connection of the controller | **OVE E 8101:2025-10-01**, ETV 2020; controller conformity per EN 60730 (manufacturer) |
| Commissioning, flushing, pressure test | **ÖNORM EN 14336:2026** |
| Acceptance and recurring inspection | Oö. LuftREnTG §§ 22, 25, Oö. HaBV 2022, **ÖNORM H 7510-1…6:2021** |
| Funding | Federal "Kesseltausch 2026", Land OÖ biomass and solar programmes, UZ 37 |

## Firing systems and chimney sweep (OÖ)

| Document | Edition / status | Planning relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Oö. Luftreinhalte- und Energietechnikgesetz 2002 | amended **LGBl. 30/2026, in force 1 May 2026** | notification, acceptance, inspection, sweeping, heating database |
| – § 21 notification | — | only from **50 kW**; a single-family boiler below that is not notifiable |
| – § 22 acceptance | — | before first operation: acceptance with trial run by an authorised person, acceptance report, data sheet (Annex 4) kept at the system |
| – § 25 recurring inspection | changed 2026 – verify intervals in RIS | as of 2020 text: >15 to <50 kW every 2 years |
| – § 49a heating database | new 2026 | new or substantially changed firing systems must be registered (includes wood boilers) |
| Oö. Heizungsanlagen- und Brennstoffverordnung 2022 (HaBV 2022) | LGBl. 39/2022 | emission and efficiency requirements, inspection forms |
| Oö. Fangverordnung | LGBl. 140/2003 | flue inspection and cleaning |
| Kehrgebietsverordnung 1999 | latest 111/2017 | which chimney sweep is responsible |

There is no separate "Oö. Kehrordnung"; sweeping duties are in § 32 and Annexes 6–7 of the
Oö. LuftREnTG.

## Funding (status 2 Nov 2026 – changes often)

| Programme | Key technical conditions |
|---|---|
| **Bund "Kesseltausch 2026"** (from 2 Nov 2026, until budget exhausted, at latest 31 Dec 2026) | only **switching away from fossil** (not wood→wood); wood central heating €6,000 flat; **UZ 37 (2025)** emission limits at full load (UZ 37 (2021): −20 %); efficiency ≥ 85 %; < 100 kW; on the list of eligible wood boilers; energy-advice protocol; **installation by a professional**; solar bonus €2,500 from 6 m² with quality label |
| **Land OÖ biomass** (until 31 Dec 2026) | switch from fossil or replacement of a boiler ≥ 10 years; type-tested; UZ 37 limits; circulation pumps **EEI ≤ 0.23**; special wood boilers only; no new builds |
| **Land OÖ solar thermal** (2026) | existing buildings, main residence; Solar Keymark/Austria Solar/UZ; operation ≥ 10 years |

No funding programme states a minimum buffer volume; use the boiler manufacturer's figure
(EN 303-5). A pure heating replacement does not trigger an energy certificate obligation in the
sources checked (the federal programme requires an energy-advice protocol instead) – unverified.
